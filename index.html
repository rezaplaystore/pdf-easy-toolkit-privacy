PDF Easy Toolkit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Everything happens on this phone: no account, no upload. Your files stay yours.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Your files

PDFs you add or create are stored inside the app on this phone and are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What the app can reach

Only the files you pick yourself in the file picker. The camera is used only while you are scanning.

Sharing

When you share a file, it goes only to the app you choose.
