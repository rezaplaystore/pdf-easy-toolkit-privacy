PDF Easy Toolkit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Your files are worked on only on this phone and never uploaded. There is no account.

What we collect

We, the developer, collect nothing ourselves: no analytics, no tracking, and no copy of your files. We have no server that receives data from the app.

Ads

Some tools (merge, split, compress, extract text, fill & sign) and removing the scan watermark offer a short rewarded ad that you choose to watch. These ads come from Google AdMob. When an ad loads, Google receives:

  • your phone's advertising ID (and app set ID),
  • an approximate location worked out from your IP address,
  • how you interact with the ad, and basic diagnostics about the ad request.

Google uses this to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. Pro subscribers see no ads.

Pro subscription

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own terms; the app only learns whether Pro is active on your Google account.

Your files

PDFs you add or create are stored inside the app on this phone and are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What the app can reach

Only the files you pick yourself in the file picker. The camera is used only while you are scanning. Internet is used only for ads, checking Pro, and the app you share to.

Sharing

When you share a file, it goes only to the app you choose.
